# Mandarin Zest pinyin practice

Live: https://antoinesjc.github.io/pinyin/pinyin
Used on: https://www.mandarinzest.com/learn-pinyin

- 21 initials (grouped by mouth position) and 24 finals, each with a recording, an English tip,
  example syllables and the sounds it's often confused with. "I know this one" progress is remembered in the browser.
- Practice: Sounds and Syllables games, 10 questions, streak, mix-ups to review.
- Bottom: HSK 1 materials (shop tag 1, WELCOME01) and an email sign-up (Start Here sheet, source `pinyin | n/45 known`).
- Recordings are in `audio/` (`s-<sound>.mp3`, `y-<syllable>.mp3`, ü written v), loaded only when played.
  Source: CNS11643 全字庫, Government Open Data License.
